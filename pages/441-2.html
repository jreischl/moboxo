The Urban Flower Field Mural

 

Spring and summer, 2014

For this project, Jeremy Szopinski and I designed and painted a mural inspired by the golden spiral on a 9,000 square foot wall of the Public Safety Annex Building, in downtown St. Paul, Minnesota. The mural faced the Urban Flower Field, a park planted with sunflowers whose seeds are organized with the flower’s head in a series of nested golden spirals.

“Urban Flower Field, a project of City Artist in Residence Amanda Lovelee produced by Public Art Saint Paul in partnership with the City of Saint Paul, 2014.”
