Thomas Liquors

Summer 2014
Designed and painted with Jeremy Szopinski. Assisted by Minneapolis College of Art and Design student interns Mirandy Lardinois, Nikki Ivanovsky-Schow, and Kathryn Warren.

Thomas Liquors, located at Grand and Prior Avenues in St. Paul, Minnesota.

East wall.

Southeast corner showing pinot noir grapes and leaves.

We also made an oil painting on a 48″ x 60″ wooden panel that was hung at the store’s entrance. The painting is a copy of a 1930 photograph taken when the business was Thomas Pharmacy.
